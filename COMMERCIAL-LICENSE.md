The full text is in [LICENSE.txt](LICENSE.txt). It is free for any purpose, commercial use
included, as long as you keep to its terms. The terms that matter most:

- If you distribute Tempestas, or anything built from it, you must release the complete source

- If you run a modified copy as a service that other people use over a network, such as a
  hosted dashboard or a managed greenhouse controller, you must offer those users its source.

For a hobbyist, a grower running it on their own site, a school or a researcher, this costs
nothing and needs no permission.

## 2. Commercial license (paid)

Buy a commercial license if the AGPL's terms don't fit what you are building, for example if you:

- sell hardware or a product that ships with Tempestas, or with a modified copy, and keep your
  changes closed;
- offer a hosted or managed service built on a modified Tempestas without publishing the source;
- combine Tempestas with proprietary code that you cannot release under the AGPL-3.0.

A commercial license removes the AGPL's copyleft obligations for the licensed product. Terms,
scope and price are agreed case by case.
