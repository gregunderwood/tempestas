# Commercial licensing

Tempestas is source available under the [Business Source License 1.1](LICENSE.txt). Most people
need nothing more than that. A commercial license is for making money from tempestas itself.

## 1. Business Source License 1.1 (no charge)

You can read, change and share tempestas, and use it in production, including to run your own
business. For example, at no charge:

- a commercial grower running it for their own greenhouses, on their own hardware or in their own
  cloud account;
- a contractor or employee installing and hosting it for one organisation's own sites;
- a hobbyist, a school or a researcher.

Four years after each version is published, that version becomes available under the GNU
AGPL-3.0-or-later as well.

## 2. Commercial license (paid)

The [Additional Use Grant](LICENSE.txt) excludes three uses. Each needs a commercial license:

- **A cloud service for others.** Offering tempestas, or a work based on it, as a hosted, cloud
  or managed service through which other people monitor or control their own sites.
- **Support as a business.** Offering support, maintenance, installation or consulting for
  tempestas to the public as a commercial offering.
- **Hardware.** Selling, leasing or distributing hardware with tempestas installed, bundled, or
  offered for installation on it.

Terms, scope and price, usually a share of the revenue the licensed product earns, are agreed case
by case.
